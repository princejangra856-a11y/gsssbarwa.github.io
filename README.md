# gsssbarwa.github.io                                                                                                                                                                  # Govt. Sr. Sec. School Barwa (Bhiwani)

Official school website project for GSSS BARWA, Bhiwani, Haryana.

## About School

Govt. Sr. Sec. School Barwa was established in 1895 and is managed by the Department of Education, Haryana.

The school provides education from Classes 6 to 12 in both English and Hindi medium.
